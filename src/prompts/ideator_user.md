Knowledge workers drowning in scattered notes and documents: Have years of Obsidian vaults, Notion exports, email archives, and meeting notes they cannot search effectively. Standard full-text search finds words but not meaning. They want to ask "what did I decide about the pricing model in Q3?" and get an answer with the source. No existing tool does this without requiring technical setup.

Personal finance trackers with bank exports, receipts, and spending data: Download their bank CSV once but cannot make sense of it without Excel skills. Want to ask "how much did I spend on food last month vs last year?" and "what subscriptions am I paying that I forgot about?". Existing tools require linking bank accounts or learning a budgeting system -- a one-time CSV import with a chat interface is the missing product.
