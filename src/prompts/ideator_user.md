## Segment pain points (use these as starting points, not labels)

Biology olympiad students (ages 15-22) preparing for IBO, national olympiads, or AP Biology:
Have to master an enormous breadth of biology at olympiad depth -- enzyme kinetics, phylogenetic

depth + evidence-based pedagogy creates irreplaceable value that a biology-aware student would pay
for or use daily.
